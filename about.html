Our Story

Rooted in Ayurveda. Designed for Modern Wellness.

Aayurastra was founded with a singular vision: to preserve and present authentic Ayurveda in its purest, most effective form. In a world rushing toward quick fixes, we return to the time-tested principles of natural healing that treat the root cause rather than just masking symptoms.

Our clinic provides a tranquil haven where experienced practitioners craft individualized treatment pathways. Every therapy, herbal formula, and lifestyle recommendation is designed to align with your unique constitution.

The Holistic Approach

How we address health across all dimensions of your being

1. Prakriti Analysis

We evaluate your natal constitution (Vata, Pitta, Kapha) to understand your intrinsic strengths and vulnerabilities.

2. Purification & Detox

Gentle yet powerful Panchakarma therapies remove deep-seated toxins (Ama) from bodily channels.

3. Nourishment & Rejuvenation

Rasayana herbs and tailored therapies rebuild tissue vitality, immunity, and longevity.

4. Lifestyle & Dietary Rhythm

Dinacharya (daily routines) and Ahara (dietary guidelines) designed to sustain balance long after your visit.
